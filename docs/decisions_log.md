# Decisions and changes

Record each change to the questions, sample or definitions with the date and the reason.

| Date | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 2026 | Local patients only | Patients based abroad visit about once a year, so a 45-day lapse rule does not apply to them |
| Oct 2026 | Lapse cutoff set at 45 days | Agreed with the clinic's lead doctor |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Relative days since first visit and first-visit month used instead of exact dates | Reduces re-identification risk; exact dates stay in a private file outside the repository |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Seasonality analysis dropped | The six-month intake window is too narrow for a reliable seasonal comparison |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Gender included for all patients; missing kept as "Not recorded" | Gender is available on most case papers; gaps handled in cleaning and the share reported |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Area recorded as broad region only (North Goa, South Goa, Elsewhere in India) | Coarse proxy for distance that avoids identifying villages. Groups under about 10 patients to be merged |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Referral source dropped | Not consistently recorded on case papers |
